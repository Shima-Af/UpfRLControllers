\section{Synthesis: four axes of augmented observability}
\label{sec:thesis_synthesis}

The title of this thesis promises \emph{advanced AI tools for augmented
observability in the 5/6G environment}. The four technical chapters can be read
individually as an energy-optimisation pipeline---profile the hardware, forecast
the load, simulate the alternatives, control the switch---but that reading misses
the argument that binds them. The binding claim is that each chapter extends what
the network can \emph{observe} along a different axis, and that the final control
results are the evidence the extensions are trustworthy.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Each module extends the observability envelope along a distinct axis.}
\label{tab:obs_axes}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Module & Axis extended & What becomes observable \\
\midrule
Profiling campaign & the unmeasurable (sensing) & per-\upf{} attributed power and QoS compliance \\
Traffic forecaster & the future (temporal) & cluster load one control step ahead \\
Digital twin & the counterfactual (modal) & outcomes under the realisation not running \\
Controllers & the elsewhere (spatial) & one site's operating experience, at another site \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Read this way the modules are not four projects but one instrument built in four
stages. Production 5G cores cannot measure the power of a single network
function: energy counters are per-package, so per-\upf{} attributed power and QoS
compliance must be \emph{inferred}. The profiling campaign manufactures that
signal (the \emph{unmeasurable} axis). Observation normally ends at the present;
the forecaster projects per-cluster load one step forward (the \emph{temporal}
axis). Choosing between two \upf{} realisations requires knowing the outcome of
the option not taken; the digital twin supplies it (the \emph{counterfactual}
axis). And a fleet of heterogeneous edge sites cannot each learn the rare
switching boundary alone; the cooperative controller lets a data-poor site
inherit operating knowledge observed at a data-rich one (the \emph{spatial}
axis).

\paragraph{Why control is the validation, not the goal.} Every one of these four
signals is inferred, not directly measured. The natural objection is that a
system built on inferred signals might simply be optimising its own fictions. The
control results answer that objection directly. A controller trained purely on
inferred attributed power, inferred future load, and inferred counterfactual QoS
was evaluated on a held-out time period against baselines tuned on the physical
ground truth. If the inferred signals were unfaithful, such a controller could
not win; it would be optimising noise and would lose on the real held-out
outcomes. Instead the cooperative controller beats every baseline, including the
best hand-tuned classical rule, by a margin larger than seed noise and at the
lowest QoS-violation rate of any learner. That result is only obtainable if the
augmented observability is accurate. Energy saving is therefore the
\emph{instrument} through which the observability stack is validated---not the
contribution in itself.

\paragraph{Standards framing.} Each module also has a natural counterpart among
the standardised analytics functions of the 5G architecture, which situates the
contribution as AI-augmented realisations of functions operators already deploy:
the profiling surrogate plays the role of infrastructure-level telemetry
(MDAF/IDAF); the forecaster, of network-data analytics (NWDAF); the twin, of a
network digital twin; and the controllers, of a closed-loop orchestration
function. The augmentation, in every case, is that an AI tool makes observable a
quantity the standardised function would otherwise have to do without.
